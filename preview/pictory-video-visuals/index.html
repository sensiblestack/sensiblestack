Your Pictory video shows the wrong screens, and one of its cards argues with your script

We gave Pictory a finished script and let it build the video. The voice read our words exactly. Everything Pictory added itself, the pictures and the on-screen cards, needed a check it did not get, and two faults reached the export.

Fix series, post three. Basis, stated plainly: Trialled it. We built a real video in Pictory's free trial on 19 November 2026 and checked the exported file against our script scene by scene. Both faults below are from that build, not from other people's reviews.

Disclosure: Pictory is one of the programmes that may pay us if you sign up through our link, at no extra cost to you. This post exists because we used it, hit a real problem, and are showing the fix.

We took one of our own written fixes, the Jotform notifications post, and turned it into a 63 second video in Pictory. We wrote the script ourselves, word for word, and told Pictory to speak it exactly. Then we let Pictory choose everything else: the scenes, the visuals, the on-screen text.

The narration came back right. The layer Pictory added on top of it did not, in two places.

First, the visuals are generic where it matters most. The scene that tells viewers to open Settings and find the Bounce List section shows stock footage of hands on a laptop. For most videos that is decoration. For a how-to video it is the content: the viewer needs the real screen, the real section heading, the real button. Pictory has never seen the inside of your Jotform account, so it filled the gap with something that looks like a screen.

Second, a template card argued with the script. Our script ends the fix with a limit: you can remove your own address yourself, and for any other recipient Jotform Support has to do it. Pictory dressed that line in its "Common mistake" card layout, which put the correct instruction inside a box labelled DON'T, next to a DO box saying "Escalate other recipients". A viewer skimming that card takes away the opposite of the point. The words were ours. The framing was the template's, and the framing won.

Neither fault is a bug. Pictory builds scenes by matching keywords in your script to its stock library, and its how-to templates add headings, step cards and warning cards of their own. That machinery is what makes a video in five minutes. It is also why the output cannot be trusted unreviewed: the tool does not know which of your sentences is an instruction, which is a warning, and which screen your viewer actually needs to see.

The fix

Four checks, done in the storyboard before you export, in this order:

  1. Read every scene, do not just listen to it. Play the preview and read all on-screen text at full size. Template cards, headings and captions are generated text, and generated text can contradict your narration while sounding fine in the voiceover.
  2. Replace the visual on every step scene with your own screenshot. Where the video tells the viewer to click something, upload the real screen (Pictory keeps an Uploads library for your own media) and use it in place of the stock visual for that scene. One real screenshot per step is the difference between a video about a fix and a video of a fix.
  3. Delete or rewrite any template card whose framing fights the script. The DON'T and DO layouts are the dangerous ones: they sort your sentences into right and wrong without knowing which is which. If a card puts a correct step in the DON'T box, the card goes, not the step.
  4. Watch the exported file once, end to end, before it is used anywhere. Two of our three faults were only visible in the render, not in the scene list.

On our own video, the Settings scene is being swapped for the real Bounce List screenshot from the Jotform post, and the Common mistake card is being removed. The video is not being used anywhere until both are done.

The short answer

If your business runs on leads, bookings and follow up, HighLevel. It is the wider tool: CRM, pipelines, funnels, websites, email, SMS, calendars and automations in one flat priced account, with unlimited contacts and unlimited users.

If your business runs on selling digital products through carefully built funnels, courses, launches, order bumps and one click upsells, ClickFunnels is the specialist, and its funnel craft and training ecosystem are still the benchmark.

If you are a one person business that does a bit of both, ask which job pays the bills. Leads and appointments point to HighLevel. Product launches point to ClickFunnels. Most service businesses we talk to are the first kind, whether they realise it or not.

Our use, and its limits

HighLevel runs the Sensible Stack checklist funnel: the landing page, the form, and the three email follow up sequence that delivers our free stack checklist. We have set up its sub accounts, built pages in its builder, wired its workflows, and watched its usage wallet tick over. That is real, daily, unglamorous use, and the scores below lean on it.

ClickFunnels we have not used to sell anything. Plenty of people have, and its reputation is earned, but our assessment of it is a research assessment: plan documents, help pages, its own pricing page, and the published experience of businesses in our audience's size range. If we trial it properly, this page gets updated and the label changes.

What each one is

HighLevel (you will also see it written as GoHighLevel) is an all in one built for agencies and local businesses that has escaped into the wider small business world: a CRM with pipelines, funnel and website builders, email and SMS marketing, calendars and booking, forms, surveys, courses, and workflow automation, all in one login, all on every plan.

ClickFunnels is a funnel builder first. It made its name, and a very famous one, on a simple idea: a small business does not need a website, it needs a sequence of pages that walks a visitor to a purchase. The current version adds a website builder, courses, a store, email and a basic CRM around that core, but the funnel is still the point.

Pricing compared like for like

Checked against vendor pricing pages and published comparisons, November 2026:

  • The entry plans sit at the same headline price, $97 a month each. What the $97 buys differs sharply. HighLevel Starter gives you the whole platform, unlimited contacts and users, and up to three sub accounts, with email, text and call usage billed on top as you spend it. ClickFunnels Startup gives you 20 funnels, 100 pages and 10,000 contacts, with annual billing bringing it down to about $81 a month.
  • At $297 a month the two diverge in purpose. HighLevel Unlimited is about scale across clients: unlimited sub accounts, aimed at agencies and multi brand operators. ClickFunnels Pro is about scale within your funnels: unlimited funnels, pages and contacts, the affiliate centre, API access and priority support, about $248 a month billed annually.
  • HighLevel's third tier, Agency Pro at $497 a month (labelled Founder on its pricing page when we checked), adds SaaS mode: reselling the platform under your own brand. ClickFunnels has no equivalent published tier, because reselling software is not its game.
  • The honest footnote for HighLevel: the subscription is not the whole bill. Sending email costs a fraction of a cent per message (its pricing guide puts email at $0.675 per 1,000 sends), and texts and calls draw down a usage wallet. Light users will barely notice. Heavy senders should do the sums before they commit.

The shape is the story: identical doors, different houses. HighLevel charges for breadth and lets you grow inside it. ClickFunnels charges for depth in one discipline and caps the rest until you step up.

Where HighLevel wins

  • Breadth per pound: CRM, pipelines, calendars, SMS, email, funnels and automations replacing four or five separate subscriptions
  • Flat pricing with unlimited contacts and users, so a growing list or a growing team does not trigger a bigger bill
  • Follow up machinery: workflows that chase a lead by email and text until a human takes over, which is where small service businesses actually lose money
  • Sub accounts: run your business, a side project, or client accounts in separated spaces under one subscription

Where ClickFunnels wins

  • Funnel craft: templates, page patterns and a decade of teaching built around one job, converting visitors into buyers
  • Selling mechanics: order bumps, one click upsells and downsells are first class citizens, not features in a list
  • Courses and launches: the platform, the training library and the community are all pointed at digital product sellers
  • Focus: a narrower tool is a quicker tool to learn, and ClickFunnels never asks you to configure a phone system

Where each one frustrates

HighLevel: the breadth cuts both ways. The interface carries a lot, the learning curve is real, and almost every newcomer spends their first week turning things off rather than on. Usage billing means the tidy flat price has a meter running underneath it, and the funnel builder, while capable, is a generalist's tool next to a specialist's.

ClickFunnels: the price buys less platform than the headline suggests. Startup caps your funnels, pages and contacts, the CRM is basic, and there are no calendars, pipelines or proper SMS follow up, so a service business ends up bolting on the very tools it hoped to replace. The famous name also carries a famous marketing machine: expect to be sold to, energetically and often.

Verdict

Choose HighLevel if your week is leads, bookings, quotes and follow up, if your contact list keeps growing, or if you are paying for a CRM, an email tool and a booking tool separately and would like one bill instead of three.

Choose ClickFunnels if your week is offers, launches and checkout pages, if you sell courses or digital products, and if the quality of the funnel itself is the thing standing between you and revenue.

Choose neither, yet, if you are pre revenue and a landing page plus an email tool would do. Both of these are $97 a month commitments with 14 day trials, and both will happily take your money before you have anything to sell. Start smaller, graduate on evidence.

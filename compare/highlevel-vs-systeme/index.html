The short answer

If you are starting out, testing an idea, or running a small list on a small budget, Systeme.io. Its free plan is genuine, permanent and covers up to 2,000 contacts, and its paid plans undercut HighLevel at every size below the very top.

If you need one system to run the whole business, CRM, funnels, calendars, pipelines, email and SMS, with no contact ceiling and room for clients later, HighLevel, and it is not close.

If you sit between the two, the deciding question is simple: is your problem price, or is it depth? Systeme.io wins on price almost everywhere. HighLevel wins on depth almost everywhere else.

What each one is

HighLevel (also called GoHighLevel) is an all in one platform built first for agencies and now used by plenty of single businesses: CRM, pipelines, funnels and websites, calendars and bookings, email and SMS marketing, workflow automation, reputation management, courses and memberships. Every plan includes the full platform with unlimited contacts and unlimited users. The tiers differ on sub accounts (separate client workspaces) and agency features, not on core tools. There is no free plan, only a 14 day trial, and phone, email and AI usage are billed on top of the subscription.

Systeme.io is an all in one built for solo operators and course sellers: sales funnels, email marketing, online courses, a blog, communities and its own affiliate programme tools, in a deliberately simpler package. It prices by capacity, mainly how many contacts you hold, and it starts with a permanent free plan rather than a trial. Email sending is unlimited on every plan and it charges 0% transaction fees on sales.

Pricing at real list sizes

Checked against vendor pricing and published comparisons, November 2026. Monthly billing shown. A realistic list for this comparison is a small business list, because that is what decides the Systeme.io plan:

  • Around 1,500 contacts: Systeme.io free plan covers you, $0. HighLevel Starter is $97 a month, plus usage.
  • Around 5,000 contacts: Systeme.io Startup is $17 a month. HighLevel is still $97 a month, because its price never moves with your list.
  • Around 10,000 contacts: Systeme.io Webinar is $47 a month. HighLevel, still $97.
  • Above 10,000 contacts: Systeme.io Unlimited is $97 a month, exactly HighLevel Starter money, and at that point you are comparing depth, not price.

Two honest caveats. HighLevel's $97 is a floor, not a bill: email, SMS, calls and AI features are charged by usage on top, and a busy business should expect that to add a noticeable amount each month. And annual billing softens both: HighLevel annual works out at roughly $970, $2,970 and $4,970 a year across its three plans, and Systeme.io annual is $170, $470 and $970 across its paid plans.

Where HighLevel wins

  • Depth: CRM, pipelines, calendars, bookings, reputation management and SMS in one login, none of which Systeme.io attempts
  • No contact ceiling: your list can grow to six figures and the subscription does not move
  • Automation: its workflow builder is the stronger of the two by a distance, across email, SMS and internal tasks
  • Agency headroom: sub accounts, white labelling and, at the top, SaaS mode to resell the platform itself
  • It replaces more tools: for a business juggling a CRM, a funnel builder, a calendar tool and an email platform, $97 can be the cheap option

Where Systeme.io wins

  • The free plan: up to 2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, one course and unlimited email sends, permanently, with no card required. HighLevel has nothing like it
  • Price at small scale: $17 and $47 a month are real business prices, not teaser rates, and 0% transaction fees keep selling cheap
  • Simplicity: fewer moving parts, a calmer interface, and a learning curve measured in an afternoon rather than a week
  • Courses at the start: a free plan that can actually host and sell a first course is rare

Where each one frustrates

HighLevel: the $97 floor is a serious commitment for a business that is not earning yet, usage charges make the true monthly cost harder to predict than the headline suggests, and the platform's size means setup takes real time. US businesses also meet A2P registration before SMS works properly, which surprises people. We run it ourselves and rate it, but we would not tell a pre revenue beginner to start here.

Systeme.io: the capacity ceilings are the business model, so a growing list walks up $17, $47, $97 steps whether it is ready or not, and the free plan is tighter than it looks, with very limited automation rules and campaigns. There is no SMS, no real pipeline CRM and no agency structure, so a business that grows into client work or appointment booking will outgrow it and face a migration.

Verdict

Choose HighLevel if your business already earns, your list is growing past the point where contact limits bite, you need CRM, calendars, pipelines or SMS in the same system as your funnels, or you can see client accounts in your future. It is the heavier tool because it is doing a heavier job.

Choose Systeme.io if you are starting, testing or deliberately keeping costs near zero, your list is under 10,000 contacts, and funnels, email and a course are the whole job. Its free plan is the best value starting point in this category, and the honest move is to let it carry you until its ceilings, not its marketing, tell you to leave.

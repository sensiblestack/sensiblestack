HighLevel is the flat price all in one: CRM, funnels, email, SMS, calendars and pipelines for $97 a month and up, contacts unlimited, built for agencies and local businesses. HighLevel vs ClickFunnels

What it isThe famous funnel builder. Superb at one job, selling through funnels, and priced like a celebrity.
Pricing shapeHigher entry price than HighLevel, for a narrower tool. HighLevel is flat priced with unlimited contacts.
The honest gapClickFunnels templates and training are excellent. HighLevel adds CRM, calendars, SMS and pipelines that ClickFunnels simply does not have.

Choose HighLevel if you need the whole follow up machine, CRM, email, SMS, calendars and pipelines, not just the funnel.

Choose ClickFunnels if funnels are the whole business, you want the biggest template and training library, and the CRM side does not matter to you.

HighLevel vs Systeme.io

What it isThe value challenger: an all in one with a genuine free tier and paid plans far below HighLevel’s.
Pricing shapeCheaper at every size, but contacts are capped per plan. HighLevel charges more and caps almost nothing.
The honest gapFor a solo founder sending email and selling a course or two, Systeme.io is hard to argue with. Agencies and high volume follow up outgrow it.

Choose HighLevel if you run clients, sub accounts, or follow up volumes that make contact caps a real ceiling.

Choose Systeme.io if you are starting out, the free tier covers you, and $97 a month buys nothing you need yet.

HighLevel vs Kartra

What it isA polished all in one aimed at course sellers and marketers, with strong page building and memberships.
Pricing shapePriced by lead limits per plan, and costs more than HighLevel at comparable scale.
The honest gapKartra is prettier and better for digital products. HighLevel is built for appointments, local businesses and agencies, and it shows.

Choose HighLevel if your business runs on bookings, pipelines and client accounts rather than course sales.

Choose Kartra if you sell courses and memberships and want the most polished storefront of the three.

HighLevel vs Keap

What it isCRM, automation and invoicing for very small service businesses. Less funnel, more back office.
Pricing shapePriced by contacts and users; a small firm can pay less than HighLevel’s flat fee, until the contact count grows.
The honest gapKeap is calmer and includes payments natively. HighLevel wins on funnels, SMS and anything agency shaped.

Choose HighLevel if lead capture and follow up are the business: funnels, SMS, pipelines, reviews.

Choose Keap if you want a tidy CRM with invoices and automation, and funnels are not how you sell.

HighLevel vs Ontraport

What it isThe automation heavy veteran: deep campaigns, memberships and payments for established marketers.
Pricing shapeCosts more than HighLevel from the first tier, with contact based steps as you grow.
The honest gapOntraport’s automation depth still impresses. HighLevel covers more of the business, calendars, pipelines, sub accounts, for less money.

Choose HighLevel if you want the breadth, sub accounts for clients, and a flat bill.

Choose Ontraport if complex automated campaigns are your core asset and you are already established enough to pay for depth.

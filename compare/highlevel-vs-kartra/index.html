The short answer

If you sell courses, memberships or coaching and you want pages, checkout, email and course delivery polished inside one login, Kartra. It is the more finished product for that job.

If you run services, appointments or clients, a local business, an agency, a trade, a clinic, a consultancy, HighLevel, and it is not close. Unlimited contacts at a flat price, proper calendars and pipelines, and client accounts Kartra simply does not offer in the same way.

If you are a one person business doing a bit of both, ask which side earns the money. Course and content income points to Kartra. Booked work and client relationships point to HighLevel.

What each one is

HighLevel (often written GoHighLevel) is an all in one built for agencies and local service businesses: CRM and pipelines, funnels and websites, email and SMS, calendars and booking, reviews, and client sub accounts, all at a flat monthly price with unlimited contacts and unlimited users on every plan. Extras such as SMS, calls and email volume are billed by usage on top.

Kartra is an all in one built for people who sell knowledge: pages and funnels, checkout, email, video hosting, memberships and courses, webinars and a helpdesk, in a noticeably more polished interface. It prices by how many contacts you hold, and each plan caps them.

Pricing compared like for like

Checked against each vendor's own pricing page, November 2026. HighLevel prices are monthly; Kartra prices are monthly with the billed yearly figure in brackets.

  • HighLevel: Starter $97 a month, Unlimited $297 a month, Pro $497 a month. Every plan includes unlimited contacts and unlimited users. The plans differ mainly on sub accounts: 3 on Starter, unlimited above that, with SaaS mode on Pro. Annual billing is priced at ten months, $970, $2,970 and $4,970 a year. On top of the plan, expect usage charges for texts, calls and email sending.
  • Kartra: Essentials $59 a month ($52 yearly) for 500 contacts and 10,000 emails a month, with a 5% transaction fee and one product. Starter $119 a month ($99 yearly) for 2,500 contacts, unlimited email and SMS, and no transaction fee. Growth $229 a month ($189 yearly) for 12,500 contacts. Professional $549 a month ($429 yearly) for 25,000 contacts.

The shape is the story. At a small list, Kartra Essentials looks cheaper, until the 5% fee and the 500 contact cap bite. At 2,500 contacts the two sit side by side, Kartra Starter at $119 against HighLevel Starter at $97, and HighLevel is already unlimited. Past that they walk in opposite directions: Kartra charges you for growing, $229 at 12,500 contacts and $549 at 25,000, while a single business on HighLevel is still paying $97, plus usage. If your list is your business, Kartra's caps are a budget line you have to watch. If your clients are your business, HighLevel's flat price is the whole point.

Where HighLevel wins

  • Flat pricing: unlimited contacts and users on every plan, so growth does not trigger a plan jump
  • Calendars, booking and pipelines built for appointment and service businesses
  • Sub accounts: run separate client businesses inside one platform, and resell it as your own software on Pro
  • Replaces the most tools for a service business: CRM, scheduler, email, SMS, funnels, reviews and websites in one bill

Where Kartra wins

  • Polish: pages, checkout and course delivery feel finished in a way HighLevel's builder often does not
  • Courses and memberships are the centre of the product, not an add on, with video hosting included
  • Checkout and affiliate management are built for selling digital products and paying promoters
  • A calmer learning curve if your whole business is content, courses and launches

Where each one frustrates

HighLevel: the breadth cuts both ways. There is a lot of platform to learn, the interface rewards time spent setting it up properly, and the headline price is not the whole bill once SMS, calls and email usage are added. Solo course sellers may find they have bought an agency machine to do a creator's job.

Kartra: the caps. Contacts, team members and domains are all metered by plan, so success arrives with an upgrade attached, and the cheapest plan is a taster: 500 contacts, one product and a 5% fee on every sale. Businesses that take bookings for clients, or run other people's marketing, will also find the client account model they need simply is not there.

Ratings by business type

HighLevel: Solo, strong fit if you sell services or bookings, heavy if you only sell content. Micro team, strong fit. Growing business, strong fit. Mid sized, strong fit, especially with several locations or brands. Agency, the strongest fit of the two by a distance. Online seller, workable for lead capture and follow up, but it is not a shop platform. Creator, poor fit unless courses sit inside a wider service business.

Kartra: Solo, strong fit for course and coaching sellers. Micro team, strong fit. Growing business, good fit until contact caps start to govern the bill. Mid sized, workable for a content led business, strained for anything operational. Agency, poor fit, no real client account model. Online seller, good for digital products, not a substitute for a proper shop. Creator, the strongest fit of the two by a distance.

Best suited to: HighLevel, agencies, trades, clinics, coaches who sell appointments, consultants, and any business that lives in its calendar and pipeline. Kartra, course creators, membership owners, coaches who sell programmes, and marketers running launches.

Verdict

Choose HighLevel if your income comes from booked work, clients or enquiries: a service business, a local business, an agency, or a growing team that needs calendars, pipelines and a CRM nobody has to count contacts in. The flat price and unlimited contacts make it the safer long term home for that shape of business, and it is the one we run ourselves.

Choose Kartra if your income comes from courses, memberships and content: you want the selling, delivery and email in one polished place, your list is modest or grows slowly, and you are happy paying more as it grows in exchange for a product that feels made for you. Read its plans in full at kartra.com before you commit, because the caps decide the real price.

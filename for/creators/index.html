Creators

Your list is the business. Protect it.

Followers are rented. The email list is the one audience nobody can algorithm away from you, which makes the email tool the most important buying decision a creator makes, ahead of cameras, ahead of editing software. The rest of the stack is a production line: ideas in, content out, sponsors and products tracked like the business they are.

Start with the list

Kit vs Klaviyo is the decision, made from first hand use of both: Kit is built for creators earning from a list, Klaviyo for sellers earning from a shop. Pick by where your money actually comes from.

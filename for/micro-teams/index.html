Micro Teams

Tools a team of five can run without an IT person.

At two to five people the founder is also the IT department, the trainer and the help desk. Software has to be adopted without a rollout plan, and the bill has to survive being read by the person who pays it. The traps at this size are minimum seat bundles, where you pay for chairs nobody sits in, and tools that need an administrator before they need a user.

Top rated for micro teams

From our twelve project and task tool reviews, scored for this size of business:

  • ClickUp: 8. One app for tasks, docs and dashboards, customisable without a manual.
  • Notion: 8. Documents, budgets and tasks in one workspace, free for one and fair for five.
  • monday.com: 8. The easiest first impression in the series. Mind the three seat minimum on paid plans.
  • Coda (Superhuman Docs): 8. Only the people who build docs pay, which suits a team where one person builds for the rest.
  • Motion: 8. If the team's real problem is five calendars colliding, this is the fix.
  • Airtable and Todoist: 7 each. Airtable when the work is really data, Todoist when it is a flow of dated tasks.

The starter stack at this size

Clients and follow up first: HubSpot's free CRM, or HighLevel if follow up is the business (see HubSpot vs HighLevel). Then one project tool from the list above, not two. Email when the list starts to matter: Kit vs Klaviyo. Hosting that runs itself if the website is WordPress: Kinsta vs WP Engine.

What to avoid

Seat bundles of five when there are three of you. Per seat AI add ons before the basics are humming. And anything that describes its onboarding as a programme.

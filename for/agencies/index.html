Agencies

Run clients, not just your own business.

An agency's software has two jobs: delivering the work, and proving the work was worth paying for. That means time and budgets tracked against clients, reporting a client will actually open, and a sensible answer to the guest question: do clients log in free, or does every client seat land on your bill?

Top rated for agencies

From our twelve project and task tool reviews, scored for this kind of business:

  • Teamwork: 9. Built for client work, and clients log in free in unlimited numbers. The specialist wins its home game.
  • monday.com: 8. The one clients understand on first sight.
  • Motion: 8. For leads and founders whose calendars are the bottleneck.
  • Wrike: 8. Depth for multi project delivery, proofing and resource planning.
  • Asana and ClickUp: 7 each. Solid delivery platforms, less shaped around billing clients.

The agency stack

Client acquisition and follow up is where HighLevel earns its keep, with a sub account per client instead of one shared mess: the full argument is in HighLevel vs its rivals. Delivery in Teamwork or your pick from the list above. Email for clients who sell things: Kit vs Klaviyo. Client sites on hosting that will not embarrass you: Kinsta vs WP Engine.

What to avoid

Tools that charge per client login. Quote only top tiers where the financial features live, without asking the seat and onboarding questions early. And reporting you have to rebuild by hand every month: if the client dashboard is manual, it will quietly stop happening.

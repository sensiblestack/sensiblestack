How it works

Affiliate links on this site are marked rel="sponsored" and pass through our own /go/ addresses, so we can count clicks and fix a link in one place if a vendor changes it. If you sign up through one, we may be paid by the vendor. What we see at our end is only that an anonymous referral signed up. We never see your name, your email address, your payment details or anything you do inside the tool.

Scores, rankings and verdicts. Every tool is scored on the same ten measures under the method on our How We Rate page.

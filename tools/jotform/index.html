The short answer

Jotform is a form builder for businesses whose forms do a job: an order, a deposit, an application with a file attached, a booking, a waiver. The footer on its pricing page calls it "the easiest online form builder", with drag-and-drop, and says it is trusted by over 40 million users. The free Starter plan includes the standard features, on Jotform's account, and then meters almost everything you would actually count: forms, submissions, views, storage, fields, payment submissions and signed documents. Paid plans from Bronze to Gold stay at one user. Several people with their own logins means Enterprise, and Enterprise is a quote.

If the form is a survey that should land in a spreadsheet, start with Google Forms and read Jotform vs Google Forms before you pay. If the form is one piece of a CRM, bookings and follow up system, HighLevel already includes forms, and buying Jotform beside it is a second subscription for a job you may already have.

What it is

Jotform's features page describes a drag-and-drop builder: add fields, change colours, embed the form by copying a code snippet into a site, and start from what it calls 20,000+ premade templates. The form templates page repeats that figure and calls it the largest selection of free form templates online. Conditional logic, in Jotform's words, can show or hide fields, send emails to certain people, and show different thank-you messages based on how the form is filled in. File upload is a built-in field. Autoresponder emails and submission notifications are features Jotform lists, not add-ons.

Payments sit in the form. Jotform's payments page says you can sell products, collect donations and set up recurring subscriptions, with 40+ payment gateway integrations, and that Jotform never charges an extra transaction fee on top of the processor you choose. The same features page is less tidy: the recurring donations card still says "14 secure payment gateways", and the recurring subscriptions card says "40+". This review uses the payments page for the count, and treats the 14 as a line Jotform has not cleaned up.

The mobile app is part of the product, not a paid extra. Jotform's mobile forms page says you can collect data offline, and that responses are saved and synced when the device is back online. The features page says the same of "Jotform Mobile Forms, our free mobile app".

Around the form, Jotform also sells apps, workflows, e-sign (Jotform Sign), tables, a PDF editor, a store builder, boards, and AI agents. This review scores the form builder a small business would buy. It does not score those extra products as if we had run them.

Pros

  • The free plan is the real builder. Jotform says Starter includes standard features, including templates and payment integrations, and that you pay to raise limits and remove branding. You can see the product before Bronze.
  • Payments stay inside the form, and Jotform says it adds no transaction fee of its own. Order forms, donations and subscriptions are the reason a seller outgrows a free survey tool. Payment submissions are still capped, from 10 a month on Starter to 1,000 on Gold.
  • Conditional logic, file upload, notifications and autoresponders are listed as product features, and embedding is a copy-and-paste code snippet. That is the practical stack for a form on an existing website.
  • Offline collection is documented: the mobile forms page says data can be collected offline and synced when the connection returns. Useful for a market stall, a site visit or a trade show.
  • Templates are abundant on Jotform's count, 20,000+ on the templates page, the features page and the pricing footer. You are editing a starting point, not drawing a form from a blank canvas, if one of them fits.
  • Leaving with the data is documented. Jotform says you can download forms as HTML and submissions as CSV, Excel and PDF, and uploaded files as one zip. That is better than a tool that only lets you look at answers on screen.
  • HIPAA has a named tier. Gold and Enterprise are marked HIPAA-available, and Jotform says eligible customers with those features on can sign a Business Associate Agreement. You do not discover the compliance gap after you have built the intake form on Bronze.

Cons

  • The meters are the product. Forms, monthly submissions, form views, upload space, stored submissions, fields per form, payment submissions and signed documents are separate caps. A busy month can trip a limit you were not watching. Jotform's pricing FAQ asks what happens if you exceed limits; the answer did not render in the page we fetched, so this review does not invent the penalty.
  • One user until Enterprise. Bronze at $34 a month billed annually does not become a two-person tool by paying more on the same plan. A micro team that wants two logins is either sharing a password or asking Enterprise for a quote.
  • Branding on the free plan. Jotform's pricing FAQ says forms and signed documents include Jotform branding until you have a paid subscription. A customer-facing order form on Starter still looks like Jotform's.
  • HIPAA is not a checkbox on the cheap plans. Starter, Bronze and Silver are marked unavailable. A clinic that picks Bronze because the form limits fit will not have the HIPAA features or the BAA path Jotform describes for Gold and Enterprise.
  • Jotform's own pages disagree with each other in small ways. Integrations are "150+" in the pricing footer and "See 200+" in the integrations menu. Payment gateways are "14" on one features card and "40+" on the payments page. Neither conflict changes the buying decision. Both mean you should read the card you are about to pay for, not a roundup.
  • Notification email can fail quietly. Jotform's help guide describes a bounce list that stops mail to an address after a bounce, and our journal walks through that guide, including the limit that the test button does not prove delivery to the recipients you configured. This review did not re-run that fault. If email from the form is how you hear about new orders, read that page before you rely on it.
  • Support quality is a 6 because we have not opened a ticket for this review. Jotform advertises 24/7 customer support, and lists dedicated support on Enterprise. The score is the published promise, not a rescue we timed.

Who it is for

Buy it, or at least open Starter, if a form is how the business takes an order, a deposit, an application or a file, and you are the person who will log in. Solo rates 8 and online seller rates 8 for that reason: the payment field and the lack of a Jotform transaction fee are the features Google Forms does not sell. A creator who only needs an email signup is usually better on the form tools inside Kit, which is why creator sits at 6.

A micro team can live with it if one person owns the forms and everyone else only receives the notifications. The moment two people need to edit forms under their own logins, the plan card says no, and the rating drops. Growing businesses and mid-sized teams should assume Enterprise and a sales conversation, not a self-serve seat price. Agencies that already run client follow up in HighLevel should use the forms in that account before they add Jotform. Agencies that sell form builds as a standalone job, and do not need the rest of a CRM, are the ones Jotform still fits, which is why agency is a 6 rather than a pass.

Skip it when the form asks questions and stores answers. That job is free elsewhere, and Jotform's Starter limits are a nudge toward Bronze you do not need to take.

Alternatives

These are the options this site already covers. Jotform's own footer lists other builders, including Typeform, Tally, Fillout and Microsoft Forms. We have not priced those, so they are not scored here.

Google Forms. The free default, already inside a Google account, with answers in a spreadsheet. Right for surveys, RSVPs and internal requests. It is the wrong tool the day the form has to take a card payment. Our comparison has the limits side by side.

HighLevel. Forms and surveys are inside the CRM, with pipelines, email, SMS and bookings, from $97 a month on Starter. Choose it when the form is the front door of a follow up system. Choose Jotform when you already have the CRM and you only need the form. HighLevel's review is from our own account. This Jotform page is not.

HubSpot. The free CRM includes a form builder, which is why a lot of small businesses never buy a form tool. It is the right start when the point of the form is a contact in the CRM. It is not the tool we would pick for an order form with a deposit.

Kit. Forms and landing pages aimed at an email list you own. Use Kit when the form's only job is the list. Use Jotform when the form's job is a payment or a file.

Make and Airtable. Jotform lists both as integrations, and it lists Zapier as a way to send submissions to what it calls 3000+ platforms. Make is the automation tool we would use once a submission has to update a shop, a sheet and a CRM in one chain. Airtable is the place to keep structured answers if a spreadsheet is no longer enough. Neither replaces the form.

Questions buyers actually ask

Is Jotform free? Yes. Starter is $0, with the limits in the pricing box: 5 forms and 100 submissions a month are the ones that run out first. Jotform says the standard features are included, and that branding comes off on a paid plan.

What should a small business budget? Bronze at $34 a month billed annually, $408 over the year, if you need more than 100 submissions or more than 10 payments a month and you do not need HIPAA. Silver at $39 a month billed annually is the small step up. Gold at $99 a month billed annually is the HIPAA plan, still for one user.

Does Jotform take a cut of payments? Jotform's payments page says no extra transaction fee. The processor you connect still charges its own rate. Jotform meters how many payment submissions the plan allows.

Which plan is HIPAA? Gold and Enterprise, on the pricing cards and on Jotform's own upgrade message. Eligible customers with HIPAA features enabled can sign a Business Associate Agreement, which is Jotform's wording, not a promise that the form makes you compliant by itself. Jotform also says to follow applicable HIPAA requirements when you handle protected health information.

Can my colleague log in too? Not on Bronze, Silver or Gold. Those cards say 1 user. Enterprise is the multiuser plan, priced on a quote, with SSO and user management listed.

Can I take my data if I leave? Submissions export as CSV, Excel and PDF, forms as HTML, uploads as a zip, according to Jotform's features page. The logic and the payment setup do not become a file another builder imports. Export the submissions while you are still a customer.

Jotform or Google Forms? Google Forms for questions. Jotform for forms that take money, files or branching logic. The full comparison is Jotform vs Google Forms.

Verdict

Jotform earns a 7.0 as a researched pick for a one person business whose forms do more than collect opinions. The free plan is honest about being a trial of the real builder, Bronze is a clear first paid step at $34 a month billed annually, and the payment and logic features are the ones a seller cannot get by staying on a spreadsheet form. The score stops there because the plan is one user, the limits are plural, HIPAA starts at Gold, and this review has not clicked through a build in an account. Open Starter, put one real order form on it, and let the 100 submission cap tell you whether Bronze is worth $408 a year. If the form is only a survey, do not pay.

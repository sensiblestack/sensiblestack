Privacy

Plain English version. Last updated November 2026.

This site is a static website. It sets no advertising cookies. Outbound links pass through our /go/ addresses. If you have accepted analytics, a click on one of those links is counted so we can see which guides are useful. We do not sell personal data. We do not collect any unless you email us.

Analytics stays off until you choose. Accept turns on Google Analytics, which then sets an analytics cookie and reports visits and link clicks to us in aggregate. Reject leaves analytics off, and no analytics cookie is set. Both choices are stored in this browser so the site can remember them, and Cookie settings in the footer lets you change your mind. We do not use analytics for advertising, and we do not share it.

Embedded videos use YouTube's privacy enhanced player, which does not set tracking cookies until you press play.

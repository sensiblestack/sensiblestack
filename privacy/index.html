Privacy

Plain English version. Last updated November 2026.

This site is a static website. It sets no advertising cookies of its own. Outbound affiliate links pass through our /go/ redirects, which count clicks in aggregate so we know which guides are useful. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and outbound links get used. It sets analytics cookies and reports back to us in aggregate form only. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share its data with anyone else.

Embedded videos use YouTube's privacy enhanced player, which does not set tracking cookies until you press play.
